Explicit Achievement-Memory PPO Network
=======================================

Inputs
------

  image observation O_t
    shape: 64 x 64 x 3

  achievement_progress_t
    shape: 22-d binary vector
    1 = achievement already completed in current episode
    0 = achievement not completed yet


Image Encoder
-------------

  O_t
    |
    v
  IMPALA CNN
    channels: [64, 128, 128]
    residual blocks per stack: 2
    BatchNorm: off
    GroupNorm groups: 1
    |
    v
  dense projection
    Linear 256 -> 1024
    ReLU
    |
    v
  image_latent z_t
    shape: 1024


Explicit Memory Encoder
-----------------------

  achievement_progress_t
    shape: 22
    |
    v
  LayerNorm(22)
    |
    v
  Linear 22 -> 256
    |
    v
  ReLU
    |
    v
  Linear 256 -> 256
    |
    v
  ReLU
    |
    v
  memory_latent m_t
    shape: 256


Shared Representation
---------------------

  image_latent z_t      memory_latent m_t
       1024                   256
         \                     /
          \                   /
           v                 v
        concat([z_t, m_t])
                 |
                 v
             shared_t
             shape: 1280


Actor Head
----------

  shared_t
    shape: 1280
    |
    v
  LayerNorm(1280)
    |
    v
  Linear 1280 -> 1024
    |
    v
  ReLU
    |
    v
  pi_latents
    shape: 1024
    |
    v
  Linear 1024 -> 17
    |
    v
  action logits


Critic Head
-----------

  shared_t
    shape: 1280
    |
    v
  LayerNorm(1280)
    |
    v
  Linear 1280 -> 1280
    |
    v
  ReLU
    |
    v
  vf_latents
    shape: 1280
    |
    v
  Linear 1280 -> 1
    |
    v
  scalar value V(O_t, achievement_progress_t)


Probe Feature Notes
-------------------

  latents
    image-only feature after IMPALA + projection
    shape: 1024

  memory_latents
    encoded explicit achievement-memory feature
    shape: 256

  shared_t
    concat(image_latent, memory_latent)
    shape: 1280
    not currently saved as a model output key

  vf_latents
    critic feature after shared_t passes through critic LayerNorm/Linear/ReLU
    shape: 1280
    this is what --feature_key vf_latents probes

  pi_latents
    actor feature after shared_t passes through actor LayerNorm/Linear/ReLU
    shape: 1024
